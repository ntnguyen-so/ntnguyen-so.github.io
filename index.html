03 / AI FOR SUBSEA INTEGRITY

Subsea Lens

Separate leak evidence
from faulty measurements.

A subsea pressure alert can signal a leak, an operating change, or a faulty sensor. Missing a leak risks environmental damage and production shutdowns; sending an inspection team to investigate bad measurements wastes time and money.

Subsea Lens helps integrity engineers assess the cause and narrow where to inspect. Three AI agents combine pressure histories, pipeline topology, operating records, and domain knowledge into an evidence-linked report with inspection priorities, uncertainties, and next checks. A separate review checks its claims against the sources.

Inspired by my PhD research on marine sensor-data quality, this full-stack system is evaluated on a simulated 11.2 km network with 112 pressure instruments, including scenarios where leaks and sensor faults occur together.

04 / GOVERNED AI WORKFLOW

Inspection Finding Triage

The model proposes.
The engineer approves.

A Python workflow that turns 21 offshore inspection findings and an equipment registry into structured draft maintenance tickets.

Deterministic checks validate evidence and coverage, calculate urgency, and apply review rules. The complete batch is written only when every finding has a valid ticket; otherwise the previous output stays unchanged.
